## Unofficial integrations

Some integrations use endpoints that are not part of a documented public API.
Those endpoints can change or stop working at any time. Using them may also
conflict with a broker's terms of service. You are solely responsible for
ensuring that your use complies with the agreements you have with each
provider.

Broker credentials and session tokens are sensitive. Keep this app on a
trusted machine, never expose the API to the internet, and disconnect accounts
you no longer use.

## Data and privacy

- Portfolio data is stored in your browser's IndexedDB.
- Broker session tokens are kept in the backend process memory and are lost
  when the backend restarts.
- The app has no analytics or telemetry.
- The chat feature sends the prompt and the portfolio context you provide to
  the selected AI provider (Google Gemini or a local Ollama instance).
